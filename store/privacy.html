Pawlingo privacy policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026 · Published by ZDNS (Pty) Ltd, South Africa (registration 2026/796623/07)

In short: Pawlingo works without an account. Your pets, journal and what Pawlingo learns about your pet stay on your phone. Recordings are only shared if you choose "Yes, I'll help", and you can switch that off or delete what you shared at any time.

What stays on your phone

  • Your pets' names, breeds and settings.
  • Your journal of translations, including any photos taken while translating.
  • The "sound fingerprints" of your pet's sounds and your answers about what they meant, which Pawlingo uses to learn your pet. These are numbers describing pitch, tone and rhythm, not recordings.

We don't receive any of this unless you opt in to sharing (below). Uninstalling the app deletes it.

What we receive if you opt in to sharing

Only if you tap "Yes, I'll help" (you can change this any time in Settings):

  • Short sound clips (up to 15 seconds) recorded while you translate. Clips can contain background sounds, including people talking, so please don't translate during private conversations.
  • The sound fingerprint and measurements of each clip, and Pawlingo's guess at what it meant.
  • Your answers about what was really going on (situation, mood, and any comment you type).
  • Your pet's species and breed, a random ID for the pet and a random ID for the app install. These are not linked to your name, phone number, email, contacts or location, which we don't collect.
  • The app version and whether the phone is Android or iPhone.

We use this only to train and improve Pawlingo's understanding of pet sounds. We don't sell it, use it for advertising, or try to identify you from it.

Picked clips and video sharing

If you pick a clip from your phone, it is sent to our server only to read the sound, and deleted straight away.

When you tap Share video, the app sends that translation's camera clip or photo (if there is one), its sound clip and the text to our server, only to make the video. The video comes straight back to your phone and the server deletes the clip, photo, sound and video immediately. Nothing from video sharing is kept or used for training, and this happens whether or not you opted in to sharing clips.

Crash reports

If the app crashes, it sends a technical error report (the error message, where in the app it happened, the app version and phone type) with the random install ID, so we can fix it. Crash reports contain no recordings, names or pet details.

Camera and microphone

While the Translate screen is open and "Catch sounds I just missed" is on, the app keeps the last 10 seconds of microphone audio in memory on your phone so tapping the paw right after a bark or meow can still translate it; that rolling buffer is never written to disk or sent anywhere, and is dropped as soon as you leave the screen. The camera is optional. When it's on, it films your pet (without sound) while the paw is lit, for your share card and share video; clips and photos stay on your phone unless you share them yourself (see Video sharing above).

Where it's stored and for how long

Shared clips and answers are stored on Google Cloud in South Africa (Johannesburg region), encrypted in transit and at rest. We keep them while they're useful for training, or until you delete them.

Deleting your data

  • In the app: Settings → Delete everything I've shared removes every clip and answer from this install, on the phone and on our servers.
  • Turning sharing off deletes anything that hasn't been sent yet.
  • Or email SUPPORT_EMAIL_TBD. Because we don't know who you are, include your install ID, shown at the bottom of Settings in the app.

Children

Pawlingo is for people aged 13 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Your rights

Under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), and the GDPR where it applies, you can ask to access or delete information about you, or object to how it's used. Contact SUPPORT_EMAIL_TBD. You can also complain to the South African Information Regulator.
