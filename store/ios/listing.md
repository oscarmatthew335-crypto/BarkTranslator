## Description (4,000 max)

Ever wished you knew what your dog's dramatic sigh at the braai or your cat's 3 AM hallway yowl really meant?

Pawlingo listens to your dog or cat, measures the real sound (how many barks or meows, how loud, pitch, tone and rhythm) and turns it into funny, fitting translations in 12 personalities. The more you tell it what your pet really meant, the better it learns your pet's own sounds.

Pawlingo is made for fun and bonding with your pet. Translations are playful guesses, not veterinary or behavioural advice.

LISTENS FOR REAL
• Tap the paw and let your pet speak. Pawlingo stops by itself when your pet goes quiet.
• It tells apart sharp alert barks, rapid yips, long howls, soft whines, purrs, trills, chirps and hisses.
• Teach it: tell Pawlingo what your pet meant (food, at the gate, walkies, attention, zoomies). It saves a private sound fingerprint on your phone, and after a few examples it starts guessing on its own.

DOG MODE AND CAT MODE: 12 VOICES
• Dog voices: Local SA Boet, Grumpy Old Man, Sassy Diva, Officer K-9, Velcro Puppy and Sir Barkington.
• Cat voices: Sir Whiskers, 3 AM Gremlin, Judgy Aunty, Drama Moggie, Laser Operative and Purr Machine.
• Add every pet in the house. Mark each one as indoors or with a garden, so indoor cats never talk about the back fence.

SHARE VIDEOS AND PICTURE CARDS
• Switch the camera on and Pawlingo films your pet while it listens, then makes a share video with your pet's real sound and an animated speech bubble.
• Replay your pet's real bark or meow any time from the Journal.

TRANSLATE CLIPS YOU ALREADY HAVE
• Pets never perform on cue. Pick a video from your library or an audio file, or share one to Pawlingo from another app, and it translates the sound in it.
• Catch sounds I just missed: while the Translate screen is open, Pawlingo keeps the last 10 seconds of sound in your phone's memory, so a meow just before you tap still counts. Those 10 seconds are never saved or sent anywhere, and you can switch this off in Settings.

ENGLISH, AFRIKAANS AND ISIZULU
Use Pawlingo in English, Afrikaans or isiZulu (Settings → Language).

SPEAK BACK AND DECODE BODY LANGUAGE
• Soundboard: real dog and cat sounds, plus a training clicker, whistle and attention cues.
• Behaviour decoder: short guides on tail wags, play sneezes, slow blinks, ear positions and pet safety tips.

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT
No account or sign-up. Your pets, journal and sound fingerprints stay on your phone. You can choose to share short sound clips to help train the translator, and switch that off or delete everything you've shared with one tap in Settings.
